Peer format

Hot Seat

Some breakthroughs only happen when you say it out loud.

One person brings a real, pressing challenge. The group gets curious rather than advisory, and the goal is to find the problem underneath the problem. Five minutes on the format, then small groups: decide who takes the hot seat, they set the scene without cleaning it up, and the rest dig in with questions before anyone reaches for a fix.

Rules of engagement
Ask good questions. Do not jump to solutions.
Talk from the trenches. Lived experience beats a newsletter.
Be alternative. The weird thing that worked is often the useful answer.
Follow through. If you commit to something in the room, do it.
Bring something specific. “I cannot close customers outside my direct network” gets you somewhere. “I need more leads” does not.
