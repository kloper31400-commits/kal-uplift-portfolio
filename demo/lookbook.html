The Founder Lookbook

86 pages, generated from a Typeform

Every page below is application data. Nobody typed any of it twice.
Founders fill in one form. The book, the directory, the match emails, the mentor briefs and the grant reports all read the same answers.
Nobody wrote any of this. Every word, number and photo on the pages below comes straight out of the application Typeform. There is no second step, no content round, no chasing people for a bio. The moment a founder is accepted into the programme, their page exists.
Why that is the whole point. A cohort book is normally somebody's week: chase 68 founders for a bio and a photo, paste them into a template, discover four are wrong, reissue. Because this one is a view over the form rather than a copy of it, a founder editing their own answer changes the page, and a cohort that grows produces a longer book on its own. The working pages at the back are filters over the same rows, which is what turns a brochure into something a partner or an investor can act on.

About

Trellis Health builds scheduling and hand-off software for outpatient care coordinators. At the pilot site it removes roughly four hours a week of phone tag per coordinator, which is the number the whole business currently rests on.

Milestones they expect to hit during the programme

Two more pilot clinics signed. A security questionnaire she can answer without a SOC 2. A repeatable first call that does not depend on knowing the medical director.

What they want to accomplish

“A repeatable way to sell into clinics that does not depend on me personally knowing someone. Right now every deal is a favour.”

Why mentorship, right now

“I can get a meeting anywhere someone knows me, and nowhere else. I need someone who has hit the procurement wall and can tell me which parts of it are real.”

What they bring to it

“I have run three clinical implementations end to end. If anyone else is selling into health systems I can save them a quarter.”
