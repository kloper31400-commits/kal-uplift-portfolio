How to structure it

Three meetings, each with a job

Mentors kept asking “so what do we actually talk about?” This is the answer, given up front rather than left to the pair to invent.

Meeting 1 · within 7 days
Discover

Get to know each other. Their company, their goals for the eight weeks, and where they are stuck. Leave with one clear priority to work on together.

Meeting 2 · within 10 days of Discover
Act

Work the problem. Dig into the priority from Discover: pressure-test the plan, share what you have seen work, and agree on concrete next steps.

Meeting 3 · by October 23
Roadmap

Zoom out. Help them leave with a plan for what comes after the programme: what to do, what to avoid, and where you can still open doors.

Why we matched you
Six years selling clinical software into hospital systems before moving to the investing side. Her blocker is the exact wall you spent those years climbing. Sector, stage and focus all line up; the deciding factor was the procurement experience.
