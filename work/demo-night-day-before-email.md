## Body

Hello,

You are registered for AI Demo Night tomorrow, Thursday, September 10, in Jersey
City. Here is everything you need to know before you arrive.

**Bring a government-issued ID.** The event is at
[Apprentice](https://apprentice.io), a secure building. Every guest checks in
downstairs at the main lobby, without exception. Your name has been sent to
building security in advance. Your ID is what matches you to that list.

**Please avoid bringing a bag.** All bags are screened at the lobby, which is the
most common reason a guest ends up waiting instead of walking in.

**Then take the elevator to Floor 38.** Name tags are laid out alphabetically
just inside the entrance. Find yours, peel it, and wear it. Nearly everyone in
the room will be meeting each other for the first time, and the name tags are
what make that easy.

**Food, soft drinks, and time to meet people**
Doors open at 6:30 PM with pizza, seltzer, and snacks. This venue does not permit
alcohol, so soft drinks are what is being served. Pitches begin at 7:00 PM, and
the room stays open for networking until 9:00 PM. Arriving early is worth it. The
half hour before the pitches is when most of the introductions happen.

**The three companies pitching**
Each founder gets five minutes to pitch and five minutes of live questions from
the audience. There is no panel, and the audience votes for the winner.

- [clicr](https://clicrapp.com) · Harrison Wilkins, with co-founder Lucas Martinez
- [mobius](https://mobiusfit.app) · Caroline Turnbull, with co-founder Julia Suozzi
- [Juricratic](https://juricratic.com) · Connor O'Dea, traveling in from Denver

More than 100 companies applied for these three slots in under 24 hours.

**Getting there**
Thursday, September 10 · 6:30 to 9:00 PM
[101 Hudson Street, Floor 38, Jersey City, NJ](https://maps.google.com/?q=101+Hudson+St+Jersey+City+NJ)
Closest PATH stop is Exchange Place

The evening is hosted at the offices of Apprentice, a TechUnited:NJ community
member building here in Jersey City. They offered the space and their team, and
the night would not happen without them.

If you need anything tomorrow, our team will be on site and away from our
computers for most of the evening. For anything event related, email
[team@techunited.co](mailto:team@techunited.co). It reaches the whole team and is
the fastest way to get an answer.

Cannot wait to see you in person.

Kennedy Loper
Head of Operations & Community
