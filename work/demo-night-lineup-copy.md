**CLICR**
Harrison Wilkins, Co-founder and CEO.
Venues and events still run on analog counters, radios, and text threads.
CLICR replaces all of it with counters that communicate and one live
dashboard: real-time occupancy, foot traffic, and crowd analytics a team can
check from anywhere and compare over time. They have counted more than 1.6
million guests, powered live crowd data for TikTok events, and ran all 215
events of Atlanta Tech Week 2026. Know your crowd. Grow your revenue.

**mobius**
Caroline Turnbull, Co-founder, pitching alongside co-founder and CTO Julia Suozzi.
The social network for fitness centered around community, not metrics. Share
your walks, your runs, your yoga class, and whatever type of movement you love
with a community that cheers it all on equally. On mobius, all movement counts.

**Juricratic**
Connor O'Dea, Founder, CEO and CTO.
Live adversarial matter intelligence for litigation. Juricratic treats a legal
matter as a game where nobody can see the whole board: fork the case, simulate
what the other side does next, and watch leverage, risk, and the best available
move shift as the facts change. Rehearse the lawsuit before you live it.

Each team gets five minutes to pitch and five minutes of live Q&A from the
room. No panel, no filter, just the founders and about a hundred people who
build for a living.
