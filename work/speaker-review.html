Nobody rewrites any of this
What the speaker types into the form is the copy that ships. The review screen is where you read it, not where you rewrite it.
Session title & what they would cover
Becomes the title and description on the published Luma event founders register through, and the headline on their one-page brief.
The three takeaways
Printed on the brief the host reads before introducing them, and used on the event page so a founder can decide whether the session is worth their hour.
Their bio
Goes onto the brief and the event page as written. A speaker describes themselves better than a programme manager paraphrasing them at 11pm.
Ranked dates & sessions offered
Drive the slot board and the capacity maths, and the date they are booked into is one of the choices they themselves ranked.

One consequence worth naming. Because the brief is generated from their answers rather than written about them, a speaker correcting their own bio corrects the brief and the event page too. There is no second copy to go and fix, and no version of the truth that lives only in someone's outbox.
