Camera Wrap — Camp Snap

A four-piece skin for the Camp Snap screen-free camera, built on the brand spectrum sampled straight out of the TechUnited mark. Two colorways, plus a test-fit sheet to check the openings against your actual camera before you commit to vinyl.

Print Sheet 1 first. Camp Snap doesn't publish a control-position drawing, so the lens, flash, viewfinder, counter, shutter and USB openings are estimated from product photography. Sheet 1 is an unprinted outline with a ¼ in grid — hold it on the camera, mark where the real openings fall, then correct the --lens-x / --lens-y-style variables in the CUTOUT POSITIONS block at the top of this file. Everything re-flows automatically.

Why the branding sits low

All the hardware on a camera this shape lives in the upper two-thirds of the front and back. The wordmark is parked in the bottom 0.78 in band on purpose — so even if your opening positions differ from the estimates above, the cuts land in open gradient and never through the logo.

The four pieces

A — side + front + side6.5 × 2.5 in
B — back4.5 × 2.5 in
C — top4.5 × 1.0 in
D — bottom4.5 × 1.0 in

Piece A wraps around the front and both ends in one continuous run, so the two seams land on the rear corners where nobody looks, and the front face has no seam at all.

Colorways

  • Sheet 2 — Spectrum. Full cyan→orange gradient field, white mark, prismatic facet overlay. Loud, photographs well, reads instantly across a room.
  • Sheet 3 — Midnight. Near-black body with a single spectrum slash and a gradient base edge. Subtler, and cheaper if you're printing on a laser.

Production

  1. Print at 100% scale — "Fit to page" off. Verify with the 1 INCH bar.
  2. Best result: matte print-and-cut vinyl with a laminate. Any sign shop takes this file directly. Ask for permanent adhesive, matte laminate.
  3. DIY: full-sheet sticker paper, cut with a craft knife and a metal ruler, then clear packing tape over the top as a laminate.
  4. Cut the openings slightly undersize and trim outward until the control moves freely. Round every inside corner — square corners are where a vinyl skin starts to peel.
  5. Clean the body with isopropyl and let it dry. Apply piece A front-face first, squeegee out from the centre, then wrap the ends. B, C, D after.
  6. Leave the lens and flash openings a touch generous — vinyl overhanging a lens barrel will flare your shots.

How to use this sheet

  1. Confirm scale with the 1 INCH bar below first.
  2. Cut out piece A, hold it on the camera, and mark the true centre of every opening. Count grid squares (¼ in) from the panel's top-left corner.
  3. Edit the CUTOUT POSITIONS block in this file — a lens 2⅜ in across and 1⅛ in down becomes --lens-x: 2.375in; --lens-y: 1.125in;
  4. Reload, re-check, then print Sheet 2 or 3.
