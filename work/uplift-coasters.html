Summit Coasters — Looking Back / Looking Forward

Ten double-sided 4″ round coasters. The navy side asks a reflection question (Looking Back); flip it over for the white side (Looking Forward). Sheets are ordered front/back for duplex printing.

  1. Print on heavy cardstock, double-sided, flip on long edge, at 100% scale (no “fit to page”).
  2. Cut along the dashed circles — or send this file to a print shop and ask for 4″ round coasters.
  3. Back-side positions are already mirrored so each question pairs up with its flip side.
