# Uplift Exit Survey — Mentees

**Goal order:** 1) funder data, 2) program improvement, 3) keep participants engaged.
**Length target:** under 5 minutes, 25 questions.
**Linking to applications:** send each founder a personalized link with Typeform hidden fields (`email`, `first_name`, `mentor`). Email is the join key back to their application (form hAbo7Jdh), so stage questions below can be compared person by person without asking anyone to repeat themselves.

Questions marked **[mirrors application]** use the exact same answer options as the original application, so before/after deltas are clean.

## Analysis notes (not in the survey)

- **Stage delta:** Q2 vs application "current stage of your company", joined on email. Report as "X% of founders advanced at least one stage."
- **Journey delta:** Q3 vs application journey question captures aspiring → active founder conversions, a strong NJEDA story.
- **Survival rate:** Q4 gives "still building" percentage.
- **Felt progress (Q8/Q9/Q10):** the statement list is built for pull-quotes in funder reports, e.g. "82% of founders say they see their next steps with greater clarity." Every answer scale is framed around growth, so mid-tier results still read as positive movement in the NJEDA report, while "A little" and "Too early to tell" keep the data honest.
- **NJ resources (Q11):** the explicit New Jersey framing exists for the NJEDA report; it replaced a generic "discovered resources" line in the Q9 list.
- **Network effect (Q14/Q15):** count plus breakdown shows whether connections came from peers, mentors, or the wider ecosystem.
- **Match quality (Q16)** mirrors the mentor survey's match question, so both sides of every pair can be compared and mismatches spotted before rematching anyone in a future cohort.
- **Q19/Q20 use an identical list** so strengths and weaknesses are directly comparable in one chart. Communication and the portal are separate items, as are the midpoint meetup and the Summit, so feedback points at one thing.
- **TechUnited questions (Q22/Q23)** measure brand lift and retention intent, the pipeline from Uplift into membership and future programming.
- The application already collected consent for aggregate reporting and public recognition of name/company/role. Q25 adds the pieces it didn't cover: newsletter, logo use, direct quotes, and 6-month follow-up.
