## EVENT DESCRIPTION

September's AI Demo Night happens to land right around Uplift Mentorship's
fall program onboarding, right when we get to formally kick off and welcome
you into the program. We saw that as a genuinely good chance to meet you in
person.

There are other opportunities to connect in person throughout the program,
but we all know how hard it can be to get everyone in a room at the same
time. So let's take advantage of this one.

There are five other onboarding sessions offered virtually. This is the exact
same onboarding, just done in person instead of on Google Meet. If you'd
rather stick to virtual, that's totally good. If you can make it out and want
to meet face to face, call this early access. You're invited to do that too.

We'll go over the onboarding deck: expectations, rules, guidelines, sessions,
mentor meetings, how to log everything, all the good stuff. Even the same dad
jokes, cued right on the slides, so I know exactly when they're coming.

Then we all get to meet each other: intros, fun facts, the typical stuff.

It's also a great chance to introduce yourself in person to Aaron Price,
TechUnited's CEO, and MJ, TechUnited's COO, before the room floods with
everybody else for Demo Night. You might see Aaron and MJ floating around
Uplift anyway, but this is a good shot at an extra hello.

Then at 6:30, the doors open to our broader community for AI Demo Night:
broader networking, the three pitches, a keynote, and the audience choice
awards.
