Microphone Flag Wrap — Uplift × TechUnited

A four-sided mic flag so both marks read on camera from any angle. Every face carries the same lockup: Uplift Mentorship Program / Summer 2026 at the top, the Uplift mark at center, and TechUnited New Jersey as presenter at the base.

Two ways to build it

  1. Insert cards (recommended) — Sheets 1 or 2. Print, trim to the crop marks, and slide into a blank 3 × 3.5 in acrylic mic flag. Cleanest result, reusable shell, survives a full day of handling.
  2. Fold-up paper flag — Sheets 3 + 4. No shell needed. Print both on heavy cardstock, score the folds, glue the tab, then add the top and bottom collars so it grips the mic instead of sliding down.

Print settings

  • Scale: 100% — turn off "Fit to page" / "Shrink oversized pages".
  • Paper: Letter. Background graphics: on.
  • Stock: 80–110 lb cover for inserts, 110 lb for the fold-up version.
  • Check the 1 INCH bar at the bottom of each sheet with a ruler before cutting.

Colorways

  • Sheet 1 — Midnight. Navy field, white Uplift mark, spectrum edges. Highest contrast under stage light and on camera. Use this one by default.
  • Sheet 2 — Paper. Off-white field, full-colour TechUnited mark. Better in daylight and for photos against dark backdrops.

Assembly

  1. Cut out both strips from Sheet 3 and both collars above, flaps included.
  2. Score every dashed line with a bone folder or the back of a butter knife, then fold each one to 90°.
  3. Cut the two collar holes slightly undersize and open them up with a round file until the mic passes through with friction. That friction is the whole mechanism — a loose hole means the flag slides down the handle.
  4. Glue strip A's tab under strip B's first face, then strip B's tab under strip A's first face, forming a 3 in square tube. Let it set.
  5. Fold the collar flaps inward and glue them to the inside walls of the tube — top collar 0.25 in below the top edge, bottom collar flush with the bottom.
  6. Slide the finished flag up the mic handle from the base until the top collar seats under the grille.
