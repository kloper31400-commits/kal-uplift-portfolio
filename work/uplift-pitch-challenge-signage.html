60-Second Pitch Challenge: Signage Set

Everything for the TechUnited Pitch Booth, on its own: the instructional sign for the booth itself, plus directional signs to point guests toward it.

  1. Signs 1 & 2 (posters/backdrops): the Founder Studio lockup, for behind the camera. Sign 1 tags "One Take. All You.", Sign 2 tags "Are You Up For the Challenge?"
  2. Sign 3 (instructions): "You've Got 60 Seconds on the Mic," at the booth, explaining how it works.
  3. Sign 4 (Pitch Substance prompts) & Sign 5 (Soundbite & Shareable prompts): at the booth, for anyone unsure what to say on camera.
  4. Signs 6 to 8 (directional): place at hallway turns and doorways leading to the booth.
  5. Print each on cardstock at 100% scale.
