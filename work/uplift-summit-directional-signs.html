Directional & Floor Signage: 5th Floor

Wayfinding signs to guide guests from the lobby / elevators to the Summit on the 5th floor. Tape to walls, doors, and elevator landings along the route.

  1. 5th Floor: place at the elevator landing or lobby directory to confirm guests are headed to the right floor.
  2. This Way → / ← / ↑: place at hallway turns and doorways; the arrow does the work from a distance.
  3. Check-In This Way →: place near the entrance to point guests straight to the registration table.
  4. Check-In: place at the registration table so it's the first thing guests see when they walk in.
  5. Print on cardstock at 100% scale. For visibility from further away, consider scaling up to 11×17 at a print shop.
