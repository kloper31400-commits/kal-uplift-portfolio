Stations — In Detail

Arrival & Networking · 4:00 – 5:15 PM

Four stations run during arrival. Everyone floats: keep energy up, point guests to whichever station they haven't hit yet, and use the lines below to explain each one. Setup throughout the venue: retractable banner, acrylic signage, and directional signage.

Station 1 — Thank You Card Station High top · self-serve

Guests write a handwritten note to someone who made a difference in their journey — a mentor, a fellow founder, someone who opened a door or believed in them first. They can hand it over tonight or mail it after.

Materials Postcards, envelopes & pens · station sign · "Need a Spark?" inspiration list with prompts for anyone stuck on who to write to.
If a guest hesitates, point at the prompt list: "Who saw something in you before you saw it in yourself?" gets almost everyone moving.

Station 2 — 60-Second Pitch Challenge Podcast room · Max runs it

The Live Founder Studio: founders record a branded 60-second video introducing their startup. One take, all you. Framed as a fun challenge, not a test — the goal is a shareable clip every founder walks away with.

Setup Device capture on tripod · step-and-repeat backdrop · mic. Flow: grab the mic → press record → approve or retake → enter your info.
Prompts at the booth "What problem are you solving?" · "Who's it for, why now?" · "One traction number."
Directional signs point here from the hallway — if the room is quiet, walk the floor and recruit founders personally.

Station 3 — No-Screen Cameras Floating · borrow & return table

Screenless cameras passed around the room — no filters, no checking the shot, just the moment. Each camera holds up to 500 photos shared by everyone who picks it up tonight.

Flow Grab a camera → snap a few candids → return it to the table.
Best results come from handing a camera to a group mid-conversation: "take one of you three." Genuine beats posed.

Station 4 — Grab Your Certificate Certificate pickup table

Completion certificates for graduating founders, laid out alphabetically. Guests find their name and take theirs — 4 cohorts complete today, the 5th by end of August.

Great photo moment — flag Alexandr or a no-screen camera when a founder picks theirs up.

Not stations — but on the floor

Check-in table. Kailan checks guests in; everyone signs the printed signature pages (mentee & mentor sign-in sheets). Sticker swag lives here too.

Jackie — content. Epic human floating around all night. Encourage guests to take content with her — quick reactions, walk-and-talks, station moments.

Food & drinks. Pizza, sparkling water, still water — plus champagne flutes with apple cider to celebrate. "Grab a slice, find your people."

Name tags. Table at check-in with its own sign: find your name, peel & wear, go meet someone new.

Pitch Showcase — The Companies

The Summit · 5:50 – 6:31 PM

Three founders selected from 12 applicants. Each gets a 5-minute pitch, 5 minutes of panel Q&A, and 2 minutes of panel feedback. The Audience Choice Award closes the night. Figures below are founder-reported.

Pitch #1 · 5:55 — Ravel Genetics Rachel Hayes, PhD · Cohort 5 · Mentor: Yuri Fiaschi

Genomics-intelligence HealthTech platform — a clinical decision-support tool, not a testing company. Ravel applies deep multi-omics analysis with a systems-biology interpretation layer to unify fragmented health data into actionable, personalized preventive-care insights, sold to functional-medicine and integrative practitioners at a $1,200 provider-ordered price point.

Stage Pre-Seed · Healthcare / Life Sciences · pre-revenue
Traction Portal live at ravelgenetics.com; platform MVP in development (beta 2026) · pilot & clinical-utility partnerships planned Q4 2026 · commercial launch 2027.
Ask Seeking $700K — funds MVP completion, pilots, and commercial launch.

Pitch #2 · 6:07 — Crewasis Sharon Joseph · Cohort 3 · Mentor: Joe Spivack

Decision-intelligence platform for enterprise consumer brands — "Jira for enterprise consumer brands." Crewasis ingests multimodal market signals (reviews, social, search, first-party data), statistically ranks opportunities with its proprietary HAZRA algorithm, and turns them into cited, auditable, executed decisions. B2B SaaS: $60K starting ACV, 70%+ gross margin.

Stage Seed · AI / ML — Enterprise SaaS · the showcase's most commercially mature company
Traction $432K ARR YTD · 4–5 Fortune-1000 logos, all paid and founder-sold (Haleon, Reckitt, Waterpik, PetIQ) · 0% logo churn · pipeline incl. Kenvue, McDonald's, Burger King.
Ask Raising $2.0M seed ($300K committed, $10M post-money cap).

Pitch #3 · 6:19 — Ticker Logan Jones · Cohort 4

Social investing app for Gen Z and younger millennials — helps new investors overcome hesitation by showing real, verified trades from peers instead of anonymous hype. Features a verified trade feed, gamified "Duels" (fantasy stocks), AI research tools, and a "Time Machine" feature (Spotify Wrapped for stocks). Live on the App Store and Play Store.

Stage Early traction · Fintech — Consumer
Traction ~800 users since October launch · first revenue from Time Machine premium · 20-person volunteer team · accelerator term sheet signed · pivoting GTM to a college campus-ambassador program.
Ask Long-term model: premium features, Ticker University, sponsored content, and an aggregated verified-trades data asset.
6:31 PM — Audience Choice Award. Audience votes for their favorite pitch; winner announced from stage to close the night.
