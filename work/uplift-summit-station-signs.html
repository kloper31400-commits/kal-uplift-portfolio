Summit Station & Inspirational Signage

A companion set to the Thank You Card Station sign, with matching signage for the other Summit touchpoints, plus a closing inspirational piece drawn from the "Looking Beyond the Program" message.

  1. Welcome / Arrival (2 copies): at check-in or the entrance, orienting guests to the evening.
  2. Name Tag Station: near the name tags, on the way in.
  3. Snap a Candid (2 copies): at the camera borrow / return table.
  4. Founder Pitch Showcase (2 copies): for the stage backdrop or easel during the showcase.
  5. Find Your Certificate (2 copies): at the certificate pickup table.
  6. "Not Built Alone" (2 copies): an inspirational closer for the stage backdrop, exit, or photo moment.

Print each on cardstock at 100% scale and place in a tabletop frame or easel.
