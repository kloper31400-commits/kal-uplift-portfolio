Write It Forward: 24" × 36" Poster

The thank-you card station sign, now titled "Write It Forward," rebuilt as a large-format 24" × 36" poster with a bigger Uplift logo and typography sized for print-shop quality at scale.

  1. Send to a print shop for 24" × 36" poster printing (foam board, poster board, or gatorboard mount all work well).
  2. Print at 100% scale — do not "fit to page."
  3. Place on an easel or A-frame at the Thank You Card Station.
