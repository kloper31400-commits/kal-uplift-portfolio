Thank You Card Station: Signage & Inspiration List

Everything needed for the Summit's Thank You Card Station: a standing sign that explains the station, and a single-page list of prompts to inspire anyone who's stuck on where to start.

  1. Sign: print on cardstock and place in a tabletop frame or easel at the station.
  2. Inspiration list: print on cardstock and place flat on the table, or in a second frame beside the sign.
  3. Print at 100% scale ("no fit to page") for accurate sizing.

If you're a founder

Think of a mentor, a fellow founder, a speaker, or someone from this summer's program.
